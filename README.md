# Optimus-Prime
Autobots transform

During my first semester of university, we had to build a line following robot that would be pitched as a child’s toy for Christmas. The constraints were fairly straightforward, the end product had to fit into a 150x150mm cube and it had to follow a black line laid out on the table.

Using an arduino uno r3, small motors, servos, leds, motor drivers, 2 9v batteries, and a lot of 3d printed parts.

During this project I learned the challenges of college level group projects. My role was to design and fabricate the toy. My friends all used cardboard and hot glue, but I thought that end product would be too amateur. That would have been a far easier think to design but I wanted to push my skills and I think it payed off well. 

Attached is my design and some pictures of the process. As I found out the hard way at 3am before the presentation… soldering irons are indeed hot.
